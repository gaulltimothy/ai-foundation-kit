# AI Foundation Kit

**Get how your business actually runs out of your head and into something AI can use.**

The AI Foundation Kit turns Claude into an interviewer for the owner of a business with roughly 5 to 200 people: a shop, a trade, a manufacturer, a professional services firm. It asks one question at a time, works from real jobs you've already finished, and writes down what it learns as plain markdown you keep.

## What it does

1. **Map.** Two or three 45-minute interviews produce a stage-by-stage map of how work moves through your business, with every decision tagged mechanical, judgment or hybrid.
2. **Context.** It writes down the facts you carry in your head: what you take on and turn away, pricing and lead-time assumptions, your glossary, what "done" means and who signs off.
3. **Skills.** It turns your most frequent mechanical step into an AI skill, tested against five of your own past decisions before you trust it.
4. **Bank the corrections.** A 15-minute weekly habit turns every mistake into one dated edit, so the skill keeps getting better.
5. **Then teach.** Once the standards exist, it helps a new hire learn the judgment, not just push the button.

## How to start

Install the plugin, start a chat, and say: **"Start the AI Foundation interview."** Stop whenever you like; next time, say "Let's keep going."

Not using Claude? The kit's page has the same kit as one copy-and-paste message for ChatGPT, Gemini or any AI chat: https://timothygaull.com/kit/

## What it runs, sends and stores

Nothing beyond your own conversation. The kit is instructions and reference text only: no code, no scripts, no MCP servers, no network calls, and no data sent anywhere. What it writes stays in your chat, or in files you choose to save.
